Checkpoint manifest
===================

Six files are released: three BatchNorm-recalibrated checkpoints and the
three un-recalibrated originals they were made from. Both are needed to
check the evaluation-mode artifact of §4.3 independently.

Optimiser and RNG state are stripped. The weights are untouched: every
tensor in each released file was verified bitwise identical to its source
after a save/load round trip.
