REACHABILITY vs PROXIMITY -- does the temporal head see the wall?
========================================================================
wall at x = 111.2; 460,320 pairs, 225,639 cross-wall

Pairs matched on TRUE SPATIAL distance, split by whether they cross the
wall. A cost that measures reachability should charge more to cross the
wall at the same spatial separation; Euclidean position distance cannot.

     true dist    pairs s/c |          temporal head |          latent L2
                            |      same   cross ratio |    same  cross ratio
     20-40      62142/21713 |      23.5    27.2 1.16 |   20.03  17.43 0.87
     40-60      54071/43397 |      35.1    45.2 1.29 |   20.52  19.46 0.95
     60-80      36724/51276 |      45.0    56.4 1.25 |   20.54  20.37 0.99
     80-100     21568/42075 |      54.5    68.9 1.26 |   20.49  20.98 1.02

  VERDICT
  mean cross/same ratio, temporal head : 1.24
  mean cross/same ratio, latent L2     : 0.96

  At matched spatial distance the temporal head charges 24% more to
  cross the wall. It has learned reachability, not proximity --
  which is why it outplans a cost built from true position, and
  why predicting spatial distance WORSE makes it a better
  planning objective.
